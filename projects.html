Systematic Review and Meta-analysis

EVAR versus Open Repair for Ruptured Infrarenal Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm

This project focuses on the comparison between endovascular repair and open surgical repair in ruptured infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm, with particular attention to hemodynamic selection bias.

What I learned: clinical heterogeneity, selection bias, evidence interpretation, and the difficulty of making conclusions from observational surgical studies.

Vascular Surgery Meta-analysis Selection Bias
MIMIC-IV Database Study

Dynamic Prediction of Postoperative Acute Kidney Injury in Surgical ICU Patients

This planned project aims to use the MIMIC-IV database to explore postoperative acute kidney injury in surgical ICU patients and identify potentially modifiable risk factors.

What I want to learn: database extraction, variable definition, model building, dynamic prediction, and clinical interpretation.

Surgical ICU AKI Prediction Model
Medical Device Innovation

Femoral Artery Compression Device after Interventional Procedures

This project explores a mechanical compression device for post-procedural femoral artery hemostasis, focusing on anti-displacement design and passive mechanical feedback.

What I learned: clinical needs can become device ideas, and even a small practical problem may contain research and innovation value.

Medical Device Intervention Hemostasis
Computational Molecular Research

Molecular Docking and Molecular Dynamics in Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm

This project explores inflammation-related targets and candidate small molecules in abdominal aortic aneurysm using molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulation.

What I learned: computational results need biological interpretation, and mechanistic exploration should not be separated from clinical disease understanding.
