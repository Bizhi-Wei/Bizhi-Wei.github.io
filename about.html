Who I am

I am Bizhi Wei, an undergraduate medical student at Pu Ai Medical School, Shaoyang University. My interests include vascular surgery, surgical critical care, evidence-based medicine, clinical database research, and translational methods.

I have worked on systematic reviews and meta-analyses, a MIMIC-IV postoperative AKI project, a femoral-artery compression device concept, and molecular docking / dynamics workflows.

Why this site exists

At the beginning, research feels distant and intimidating. Papers, data, revisions, and failed attempts slowly turned into a kind of self-training.

I keep this site to record that process honestly — technical when needed, reflective when that is more accurate. The 3D layer is not decoration; it is a reminder that methods and selection shape what we think we see.

Research is not only about producing results. It is also about learning how to ask better questions — especially whether two groups were already different before treatment was chosen.
