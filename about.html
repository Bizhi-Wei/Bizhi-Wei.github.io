About This Blog

A personal space for research, medicine, and growth.

This page is not designed as a formal CV. It is a more relaxed record of how I learn, struggle, reflect, and grow as an undergraduate medical student.

Who I Am

I am Bizhi Wei, an undergraduate medical student with interests in vascular surgery, surgical critical care, evidence-based medicine, clinical database research, and translational medicine.

My current academic exploration includes systematic reviews and meta-analyses, MIMIC-IV database studies, medical device innovation, molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulation.

Why I Built This Site

At the beginning, research can feel distant, difficult, and even intimidating. But as I gradually worked through papers, data, revisions, and failed attempts, I realized that research is also a process of self-training.

I built this site to record that process honestly. Some posts may be technical. Some may simply be reflections after a long day of reading, writing, or debugging.

Research is not only about producing results. It is also about learning how to ask better questions.

Current Interests

Clinical Research

I am interested in clinically meaningful questions, especially those related to surgery, postoperative complications, emergency conditions, and prognosis.

Evidence-Based Medicine

Systematic reviews and meta-analyses helped me understand how clinical evidence is collected, evaluated, synthesized, and interpreted.

Medical Data Science

Database studies, especially using MIMIC-IV, allow clinical problems to be explored through structured data and prediction modeling.

Translational Exploration

Molecular docking and molecular dynamics give me a way to connect disease mechanisms, molecular targets, and candidate compounds.
