P1

Published · Annals of Vascular Surgery · 2026

Hemodynamic selection bias in observational comparisons of EVAR versus open repair for ruptured infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Wei B, Yuan C, Li M. Annals of Vascular Surgery. 2026. Journal Pre-proof.

Six studies (1,035 patients) in the primary 30-day mortality analysis. EVAR associated with lower mortality (OR 0.59, 95% CI 0.44–0.80), but no study met relatively balanced treatment-allocation criteria.
